## What's new in 0.3.0

**Smarter on-device AI**
- New malware model with 14 features, trained on 14,515 real Windows programs. On real apps it
  had never seen, false alarms fell from 3.27% to 0.88%.
- Every detection explains why, for example "packed code, risky APIs: WriteProcessMemory,
  not digitally signed".
- See a verdict yourself without changing anything: `the-nex --scan-file <file or folder>`.

**Safer with running programs**
- Programs that were already running when protection starts only raise an alert.
- A newly started program is stopped only when the AI is at least 90% sure. Below that you
  get an alert instead.
- The Nex never acts on its own process or whatever launched it.

**AegisMesh (new page in the app)**
- A simulation of a silent-SOS wearable: a secret tap rhythm sends an encrypted SOS across a
  relay mesh, with nothing left on the phone. No radio, network or emergency service is ever
  used.
- Camera watch: hold up an emergency hand sign (the Signal for Help) or a facial expression
  (double wink), or teach it your own sign. Recognition runs on your device and the page blocks
  every off-device connection.

**New dashboard**
- Protection score, threat tiles, a 7-day threat chart and an AegisMesh tile on the overview.

Every build is tested on Windows and macOS runners: unit tests, a live end-to-end check, AI
false-positive gates on each runner's own programs, and a full install and uninstall.
