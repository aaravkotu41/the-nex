Hackathon project, as is

The Nex is a hackathon project. It is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind. It has no lab certification and may miss threats or flag harmless files.

Quarantine

The Nex can move files into quarantine. It does not delete them on its own, and you can restore a quarantined file from the app.

Do not rely on it alone

Do not use The Nex as your only protection. Keep your operating system updated, keep backups and use other security measures as well.
