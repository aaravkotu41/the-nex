No guarantees

The Nex is a hackathon project. You get it as it is, with no warranty of any kind. No lab has tested it. It can miss harmful files, and it can flag safe ones.

What it can do to your files

The Nex can move a suspicious file to a safe place called quarantine, and stop a program. It does not delete your files on its own. You can put a file back from the app.

Do not rely on it alone

Do not use The Nex as your only protection. Keep your computer updated, keep copies of your files, and use other protection too.

Made with other people's work

The Nex uses this free software and these services:

  • Tauri: MIT or Apache-2.0
  • three.js: MIT
  • MediaPipe Tasks Vision and the hand and face models: Apache-2.0
  • Supabase: hosted service for sign-in and device sync
  • Gravatar: hosted service for profile photos
