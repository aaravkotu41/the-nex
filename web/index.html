What it does

Trick zip files
Some zip files are built to fill up your computer when opened. The Nex spots them before you open them.
New programs
When a new program from an unknown maker shows up, The Nex gives it a risk score and tells you why.
Ransomware
Ransomware locks your files and asks for money. The Nex puts out decoy files and warns you if something tampers with them.
Dangerous commands
It watches for hidden commands that attacks often use.
Nothing is deleted without you
Suspicious files are moved somewhere safe. You can put any of them back with one click.
Quick check any time
Start a quick scan from the app, or from the menu bar on a Mac.

How it is different

Many protection tools send your files, or fingerprints of them, to a company to be judged. The Nex does not. It works without an account and without the internet.

It always explains itself. You see the risk score and the reasons, so you can decide for yourself.

It makes mistakes. In our tests it wrongly flagged about 1 in 200 safe programs. That is how often it is wrong about safe programs, not how much harmful software it catches. No independent lab has tested it.

With an optional account you can see all your computers in one place and start a scan on another one. The Nex also includes AegisMesh, a demo of a wearable that calls for help. It is only a simulation.

Privacy

This website does not track you: no cookies and no visitor statistics. The app never uploads your files. If you choose to sign in, a short summary of each computer is stored online so you can see it from the others. The privacy page has every detail.
